ANIMAL KINGDOM

ACT ONE

In the heart of the great Savanna Kingdom, where the tall acacia trees touched the sky and the river sang all day, lived King Leo the Lion, whose golden mane was famous across all lands, but age had finally caught him and his roar that once shook mountains had become a weak cough that even the rabbits could not hear anymore.

The news of the King's sickness spread like fire through the dry grass, reaching the deepest holes of the forest and the highest branches, causing fear and whispers among all the animals who had never known life without the strong and just rule of King Leo and his family for many generations.

Elephant Kobo, who was the strongest and biggest of all animals, trumpeted loudly and said that power belongs to those with strength and size, and therefore he should be the next King to protect the kingdom from the hyenas and hunters that were coming closer every day.

Monkey Miko jumped from tree to tree laughing at Elephant, claiming that strength without brain is useless like a big rock in the river, and that only intelligence and speed should rule, which he had more than anyone else, so he should be crowned as the new King immediately.

While all animals were shouting and fighting in the palace square, old Tortoise Tatu who was known as the slowest but wisest animal in the kingdom was quietly watching everything from under a mango tree, knowing that this fight for power would destroy everything their fathers built if someone did not stop it quickly.

When Tortoise Tatu finally reached the middle of the fighting crowd after almost an hour of slow crawling, he did not shout or push anyone, he simply sat down and cleared his old throat and said in a very calm and deep voice that shook the ground not because it was loud but because it was full of truth and history, that my children why are you fighting for a chair that will burn all of us if we continue like this, have you forgotten that Lion King is still breathing and watching us disgrace his legacy, have you forgotten that Elephant you cannot climb trees and Monkey you cannot carry a river, and Parrot you cannot fight a hyena, so how can one of you alone be King of all, and immediately the entire square became completely silent as if the wind itself stopped to listen, because for the first time that morning someone was speaking sense instead of pride and greed, and even Elephant Kobo who was about to hit Monkey with his trunk stopped mid-air and looked down at the small Tortoise with tears in his big eyes, realizing that his strength was nothing without the wisdom to use it properly for the good of everyone in the kingdom and not just for himself and his family alone.
